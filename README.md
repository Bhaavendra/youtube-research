# NuYouth Content Studio (@Nuyouthishwarisahu)

Interactive No-Talking YouTube Shorts & Follow-Along Video Planner for **Nuyouth - ishwari sahu**.

## Live App
- **GitHub Pages URL:** https://bhaavendra.github.io/youtube-research/

## Features
- **Idea Bank (25+ Topics):** Categorized across Posture & Alignment, Gut Health & Belly, Women's Health, Daily Pain Relief, and Face Yoga.
- **Shot-by-Shot & Text Overlays:** Silent video shoot guides with camera angles, visual actions, screen text overlays, music cues, and a shoot countdown timer.
- **Ready Pictures & Thumbnails (`assets/`):**
  - `assets/wall-test.jpg` — Fat Belly vs Posture Belly (9:16 Short Hook)
  - `assets/3-exercises.jpg` — 3 Fixes for Lower Belly Pooch (9:16 Pose Card)
  - `assets/thumbnail.jpg` — 10 Min Posture Fix (16:9 YouTube Thumbnail)
- **Visual Hook & Text Builder:** Generates 5 silent scroll-stopping visual hooks in Hinglish and Hindi.
- **7-Day Shoot Plan:** Balanced weekly schedule saved locally in browser storage.
